## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** Urban track cyclists, criterium racers, and fixed-gear enthusiasts seeking premium, handcrafted Italian Columbus steel and Toray aerospace-grade carbon machines.
- **Secondary:** Atelier collectors and performance bike builders seeking bespoke framesets, deep carbon wheelsets, and race-tuned track components.
- **Administrators & Atelier Staff:** Store operators managing inventory, processing orders, reviewing analytics, and curating product dossiers.

## Product Purpose

Dosnoventa is a boutique digital atelier and direct-to-rider e-commerce destination for Barcelona-engineered fixed-gear bicycles. It enables riders to explore technical specifications, configure custom track setups, read verified buyer reviews, and purchase complete machines and components with worldwide freight. Success means delivering a digital experience that reflects the precision, velocity, and craftsmanship of the physical bicycles.

## Positioning

Boutique European craftsmanship meeting aggressive Barcelona street culture. Unlike mass-market single-speed manufacturers, Dosnoventa designs race-proven framesets fabricated with authentic Italian Columbus Spirit triple-butted steel and Toray T800 monocoque carbon, engineered for high-wattage criterium sprints and urban velocity.

## Operating Context

- **Usage Scenes:** High-speed street commuting, criterium racing, velodrome track sessions, and atelier bike builds.
- **Digital Context:** Responsive web application accessed across desktop workstations and mobile devices at trackside, workshop, or on the go.
- **Theme Adaptability:** Full support for both a crisp, minimalist studio Light Mode (default) and an immersive editorial Dark Mode.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Dynamic Catalog & Shop:** Multi-category browsing (Bikes, Frames, Components, Apparel & Goods) with price-bracket filtering, star ratings, and infinite scroll.
- **Verified Buyer Dossiers & Reviews:** Granular product reviews and community feedback tied to verified customer purchases.
- **Custom Bike Configurator:** Modular selection of frames, wheelsets, handlebars, saddles, pedals, and gear ratios with live price aggregation.
- **Order Processing & Invoicing:** Complete cart checkout supporting Credit Card, COD, and local e-wallets, automated status notifications, and downloadable PDF receipts.
- **Administrative Suite:** Real-time revenue analytics, order status dispatch workflow, user management, and product catalog management.
- **Technical Constraints:** MERN stack architecture (React 18 + Vite on frontend, Node.js + Express on backend, MongoDB Atlas database with strict TLS).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** DOSNOVENTA FIXED GEAR BICYCLES — BARCELONA.
- **Visual Identity:** Editorial minimalism, stark Swiss/European typography (Helvetica Neue, clean sans-serifs, uppercase tracking), high-contrast palette with signature Vermilion Crimson accents (`#C83226`).
- **Tone of Voice:** Direct, confident, technical, and authentic to track and street riding culture. No generic marketing fluff.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Asset Library:** Curated high-resolution studio lookbooks, photography of Barcelona atelier framesets, Tokyo carbon monocoques, Detroit pursuit frames, and carbon track wheels located in `/public/images`.
- **Live Database:** Populated MongoDB Atlas database containing complete product models, customer orders, reviews, and configurator component specifications.

## Product Principles

1. **Velocity Without Clutter:** Fast, distraction-free interactions that mirror the simplicity and purity of a brakeless fixed-gear bicycle.
2. **Technical Transparency:** Every machine's metallurgical composition, tube geometry, gear ratio, and weight must be presented with precision.
3. **Dual Atmosphere:** Natural readability in bright studio light, commanding presence in dark velodrome atmospheres.
4. **Authenticity Over Novelty:** Prioritize genuine cycling performance and engineering truth over superficial e-commerce gimmicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Responsive typography and high-contrast color tokens adhering to WCAG 2.1 AA standards in both light and dark themes.
- Clear visual focus states, descriptive form labels, and tactile feedback for screen readers across checkout and configurator workflows.
